# Action, not decorative acting

## Storyboard contract

Keep a project-local table or JSON with narration, source evidence, before/action/after, meaning, clip and inspected timing. A shipping example could be `parcel outside truck → paws load parcel → parcel inside → shipped goods`. A return example could be `return bundle in sales area → paws put bundle in return tray → separately held returns → subtract labelled quantity`. These are examples, not business rules.

Use one short action per generated shot. For long narration, cut between distinct relevant actions without slowing the character. For exact numeric lessons, locally label a prop as a quantity bundle; never silently treat an AI-generated object count as a correct count. A symbolic tray or folder is not an actual product UI.

## Review checklist with observed failure modes

- **Loading:** the first several seconds can be hesitation. Include the actual placement, not just lifting the box.
- **Returns:** a two-object request may move only one. Use a labelled bundle or separate verified actions. Do not claim both moved.
- **Adding value:** blocks may duplicate or change color. Exclude that portion, preserve an understandable before/after state and relabel only if the surviving action is honest.
- **Fees:** a kitten may sniff/eat coins instead of removing them. Reject the clip as deduction evidence. A verified document transfer can represent a clearly labelled cost bundle.
- **Documents:** moving a paper is not copying it; looking between two papers is not physically aligning them or approving them.
- **Monthly evidence:** taking papers OUT of a folder can demonstrate inspection, not collection INTO it.
- **Contracts/calendar:** holding/checking a card is not inserting it into a holder. Label the action actually seen.
- **Cancellation:** lifting and then returning the bundle to the same tray is not subtraction. Exclude the reversal or use another honest demonstration.
- **Money pouches:** inspect direction, duplicates and deformations through the exact out-point.
- **Journals:** filing/stamping a prop can illustrate preparation or recording. It does not prove a software API sent a voucher, nor that cash moved.

Create contact sheets at sufficient intervals and examine ambiguous moments more closely. Coarse sheets may miss a transient morph; review suspicious spans at finer sampling or playback. Save the observed completion time and allowed trim, not just `PASS`. Use a short representative assembled sequence before rendering the whole film. Paid resubmission is not the default error recovery.

## Continuity

Keep recurring source/destination colors and source labels consistent. Frame the face, both paws and the entire path. Do not place overlays over the contact point. Show one active meaning at a time; a three-card text list over the lower half hides the action. Synchronize local counters with completed moves, using exact source-time-to-scene-time mapping.
